RAVEN — Eurostile / Microgramma candidates

Target look: wide/squat, thin monoline strokes, flat-topped squared R, E with a short middle bar, squared letterforms. Watch these letters:

  • R — is the bowl flat-topped/squared (good) or rounded (bad)?
  • E — is the middle bar shorter than top/bottom (good)?
  • overall — wide & monoline & squared?
